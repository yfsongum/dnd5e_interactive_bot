Short Answer

Grappling restricts movement; it doesn't deal damage.

Step-by-step Breakdown
  • 1)Use Attack action to grapple (special melee attack).
  • 2)Roll Athletics (STR).
  • 3)Target contests Athletics (STR) or Acrobatics (DEX).
  • 4)If you win: target's speed becomes 0 (Grappled).
  • 5)Grapple ends when conditions are not met.
